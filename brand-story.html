OUR HERITAGE

Born in Holland, built for beautiful hair.

For more than a century, VR2C Haircosmetics has combined salon insight, scientific exploration, and family-led care to create formulas that elevate healthy hair and confident routines.

1922 Founded in Holland
OUR STORY

Rooted in artistry, guided by science.

VR2C began with a simple promise: haircare should feel luxurious, work beautifully, and respect the health of the scalp and strands underneath. What started in a modest Dutch salon evolved into a modern hair science brand trusted by stylists and clients worldwide.

We pair a century of salon experience with conscious mineral-rich formulations designed to care deeply while delivering visible results. Every formula is built around balance, nourishment, and long-term hair confidence.

OUR VALUES

Family-led care

We build trust through honest formulas, personal guidance, and a deep connection to the salons we serve.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Beauty with balance

Brilliance should never come at the cost of scalp comfort or strand resilience. We craft for both.

OUR COMMITMENT

Responsible science

Our research focuses on sustainable ingredients, scalp health, and clean performance with premium results.

OUR FUTURE

Modern salon care

We continue evolving with expressive hair rituals that keep salon expertise accessible, refined, and effective.

VR2C Haircosmetics is a premier family-owned company dedicated to professional hair care since 1922. Made with love and scientific passion for beautiful hair.
