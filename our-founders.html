Founder & Company Story

VR2C is built on professional beauty expertise, scientific knowledge, and business leadership.

Our vision is to transform skin and hair care by providing advanced, high-quality products that address real consumer needs.

We are dedicated to establishing new benchmarks for safety, efficacy, and trust, creating a time when beauty and science support healthier, more confident lives.

✦ One Team · Three Perspectives Beauty · Science · Leadership

Our founders have decades of experience in beauty, pharmaceutical R&D, finance, operations, and entrepreneurship, united by a shared commitment to high-quality skin and hair care. They have led product innovation, market growth, and business growth for leading organizations, introducing award-winning solutions and scaling companies to national prominence. Their leadership has produced best-selling salon products and patented formulations.

Venky Charan Mohan, BS

Co-Founder & Board Director
Professional Beauty & Education Leader · 27+ Years Experience

Venky Charan Mohan has more than 27 years of professional experience in the hair and beauty industry.

His career spans salon ownership, professional hairdressing, technical training, product evaluation, R&D support, and national education. He has worked with leading organisations such as L’Oréal India, Revlon Professional, Cavinkare, and VLCC, and has trained beauty professionals throughout India.

Venky contributes hands-on industry knowledge to VR2C, focusing on hair care, product performance, professional education, and consumer needs.

At VR2C, Venky uses his extensive industry experience to lead product strategy, oversee the development and positioning of new offerings, and ensure formulations meet today’s consumer needs. He also directs the company’s professional education initiatives. He actively shapes operational practices and quality standards, ensuring every aspect of product delivery reflects VR2C’s commitment to innovation and excellence.

Dr Lakshmi Raghavan, PhD

Consultant & Board Member (Co-Founder & Board Director)
Pharmaceutical R&D & Formulation Scientist · 25+ Years Experience

Dr Lakshmi Raghavan brings more than 25 years of experience in pharmaceutical research and development, specialising in formulation development, analytical science, topical and transdermal technologies, and product evaluation.

His extensive scientific background provides VR2C with valuable expertise in formulation, product development, analytical evaluation, and scientific testing.

As Co-Founder and Board Director, Dr Raghavan draws on his broad pharmaceutical R&D experience to advance VR2C’s commitment to scientific innovation and quality in skin and hair care. Through advanced formulation techniques, rigorous analytical testing, and a data-driven approach to product development, he helps VR2C deliver innovative, safe, and effective solutions.

Radhika Raghavan, MS

Co-Founder & Board Director
Finance, Operations & Strategic Growth · 25+ Years Experience

Radhika Raghavan brings more than 25 years of experience in finance, human resources, business operations, and entrepreneurship. Her expertise spans financial management, organisational development, people leadership, and operational strategy.

At VR2C, Radhika leads business strategy, organisational development, operations, and long-term growth. She drives data-informed expansion, nurtures key partnerships, and implements best-in-class operational processes.

Her extensive business and leadership experience complements Venky’s professional beauty expertise and Dr Lakshmi Raghavan’s scientific expertise, strengthening VR2C’s multidisciplinary approach to innovation and growth.
